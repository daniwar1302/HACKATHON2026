How this works

This page stands in for a Quest/Pico. It streams CONTROLLER_MOVE at 30 Hz to teleop's websocket, the same as a headset does. 1) Start teleop on waldo (--input-mode=controller --motion). 2) Accept the cert once at the teleop page. 3) Connect here: the hands stream at teleop's neutral pose. 4) Press r in the teleop terminal. The arms then follow these controls. Pose changes are rate-limited (0.25 m/s, 60°/s), so slider jumps glide.
